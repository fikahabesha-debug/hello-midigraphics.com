MIDI GRAPHICS WEBSITE
====================

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract midi_graphics_website.zip.
2. Open the midi_graphics_website folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in your browser.

FILES
- index.html: responsive website
- assets/midi-graphics-logo.png: your uploaded Midi Graphics logo
- assets/profile-photo.png: your uploaded profile portrait

BEFORE PUBLISHING
- The portfolio cards are sample concept panels, not uploaded client projects. Replace them with your actual work when ready.
- Replace hello@midigraphics.com in index.html with your real business email address.
- Add your real social links if you want them shown.
- The contact form opens the visitor's email app; it does not send/store messages by itself.

PUBLISHING
You can upload the folder contents to a static hosting service such as GitHub Pages, Netlify, or your web host. Keep the assets folder next to index.html.
